The other half of the shop

Fish, ponds & small mammals

Harris in Wonderland started as a fish house. The Canton floor still runs freshwater and marine tanks, corals and inverts, pond work, and rotating small mammals — sugar gliders when they have them. Birds can be ordered; they are no longer kept on the floor.

Freshwater

Community fish and the livestock that keeps a tank stable. Ask what came in that week rather than shopping from a frozen list.

Marine & coral

Saltwater fish, corals, and invertebrates when the systems are holding them. Call if you need a specific coral.

Ponds

The shop will install a pond, stock it with fish and plants, and stay on the line for questions and maintenance.

Sugar gliders

A long-running specialty when animals are available. Talk through diet, cage, and colony before money moves.

Other small mammals

Rotating inventory. The shop’s center of gravity is still herps and fish.

Birds

No longer kept in the store. They can still be ordered. Call with the species and the timeline.
