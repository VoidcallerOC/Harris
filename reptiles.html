Case notes

Reptiles & amphibians

Hardy starter animals next to unusual morphs. Stock moves by the afternoon. Call before you drive for a named animal.

The old site listed 2015 prices. That list is retired. Live inventory is on the rack, not on a webpage.

Snakes

Ball pythons and designer morphs, corns, western hognose, milks and kings, sand boas, carpets, bloods, rat snakes.

Lizards

Bearded dragons, leopard geckos, blue-tongue skinks, rotating unusual lizards.

Turtles & tortoises

Red-foots and other chelonians when available, plus lighting and substrate that match the species.

Amphibians

Dart frogs, pacman frogs, humidity specialists, live plants when staged.

Feeders

Pinkies through rats, crickets, roaches. Regulars treat this as a destination.

Breeding projects

In-house and local CB stock. Ask Adam or Ashlee what is on the rack this week.

How to buy here

The right animal. Not the biggest ticket.

Staff will shrink a purchase if the enclosure or the feeder size is wrong. Bring photos of your setup. If you do not have one, start with the box and the heat.

Ask on the phone

Species or morph · sex and age · same-day enclosure · feeder size · a hold. If it is for a birthday program, say that first.
