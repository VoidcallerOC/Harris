Beginner chooser

Who wants a reptile for a pet?

Copied from the old Harris chooser: hardy herps first, unusual morphs after you have the box and the heat.

Is a snake the right pet?

Corn snakes and king snakes

Beginner recommendations from Harris in Wonderland. You will need a secure cage, heat, a water bowl, bedding, a hide, and the care sheet for that animal. Ball pythons and boas are other considerations, not the first snake if you have never held one.

Is a lizard the right pet?

Leopard geckos and bearded dragons

Most lizards are nervous and would rather be observed. If you want to handle, these two are the exceptions they name. Tank, heat, UV, water, bedding, care sheet.

Turtle or tortoise?

Red-foot tortoises

Active, personable, hardy, and seriously long-lived. Voracious eaters and fairly messy. Harris recommends red-foots (and Russians) for a first tortoise if you will actually keep up with the maintenance.

Is an amphibian the right pet?

White’s treefrogs

Fresh, clean water is vital. Stagnant water turns toxic. Harris recommends White’s treefrogs and Argentine horned frogs for the beginner. Red-eyed treefrogs, mantellas, and dart frogs come later.

How to buy here

The right animal. Not the biggest ticket.

Staff will shrink a purchase if the enclosure or the feeder size is wrong. Bring photos of your setup. If you do not have one, start with the box and the heat.

Ask on the phone

Species or morph · sex and age · same-day enclosure · feeder size · a hold. If it is for a birthday program, say that first.
