Is this the right pet?

Beginner chooser.

Copied from the shop’s own reptiles page: what they actually recommend to start, not a chain “starter kit.”

Snakes

Corns and kings

Easy to keep clean, easy to feed, a tight lid (they will find a gap). Heat, water, hide, bedding. Look for good weight, regular feeding, scales that lie flat. Ball pythons and boas if you are ready for that conversation. If you will not hold a snake, or cannot pay for the setup, skip it.

Corn care sheet · King care sheet

Lizards

Leopard gecko or beardie

Most lizards would rather be watched than handled. Budget for cage, heat, UV, water, bedding. Look for an animal that eats, eyes open, muscle and fat on the body. Ready to advance: veiled chameleons, uromastyx, monitors — ask first.

Leopard gecko · Bearded dragon

Shells

Red-foot tortoise

Active, personable, hardy, and really long-lived. They eat every day and get messy. Aquatic turtles need big water changes. Be honest about decades of maintenance. Shop beginner list: red-foots, Russians, red-eared sliders.

Red-foot notes

Frogs

White’s treefrog

Fresh water is not optional. Stagnant bowls go toxic. White’s and Argentine horned frogs for people starting out. Red-eyes, mantellas, darts when you are ready for humidity and detail.

White’s care sheet

Captive-bred over wild-caught, as often as they can get it. Crocodilians: no, not likely.
