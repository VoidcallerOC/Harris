Herps

Reptiles & amphibians

A large share of the floor is dedicated to reptiles, amphibians, and the hardware that keeps them alive. Hardy starter animals sit next to unusual species and color morphs. Stock moves. Call before you drive for a specific animal.

The old site listed individual animals with 2015 prices. That list is retired on purpose. Live inventory changes by the week. Phone: (860) 674-0160.

Snakes

Ball pythons and designer morphs, corn snakes, western hognose, milks and kings, sand boas, rosy boas, carpet and blood pythons, rat snakes, and unusual colubrids.

Lizards

Bearded dragons, leopard geckos and other eublepharids, blue-tongue skinks, and rotating unusual lizards for keepers past the first enclosure.

Turtles & tortoises

Red-foots and other chelonians when available, plus the filters, lighting, and substrate that match the species.

Amphibians

Dart frogs, pacman frogs, and other humidity specialists. Live plants and bioactive pieces when staged.

Feeders

Frozen rodents from pinkies to rats, crickets, roaches, and other live foods. Regulars treat this as a destination even when they are not adding an animal.

Breeding projects

Animals bred in-house or by local people the shop will stand behind. Ask Adam or Ashlee what is on the rack that week.

How to buy here

We would rather send you home with the right animal.

Reviews keep circling the same point: staff will shrink a purchase if the enclosure, the feeder size, or the keeper’s schedule is not ready.

Bring photos of your current setup if you already have one. If you do not, start with the box and the heat — the animal is the last piece.

What to ask on the phone

Species or morph · sex and age if it matters · whether you need an enclosure built out the same day · feeder size · any hold. If it is for a birthday program, say that first.
