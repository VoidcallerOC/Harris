    House rules

    Husbandry first.

    Adam Harris has kept and bred reptiles for more than twenty years, with field time in Costa Rica, Thailand, Australia, South Africa, and the Bahamas. Seth built the original shop as a fish-keeper and taught high-school biology for 31 years.

    Many animals are captive-bred here or by local people the shop will stand behind. Frozen rodents, crickets, and roaches are why regulars drive even when they are not adding a new animal.

    Honest setups

    Enclosures, heat, UV, substrate, plants, food. They size the tub before they size the receipt.

    Rare on the wall

    Reviewers mention animals they have never seen at a chain — morphs, odd colubrids, well-kept giants.

    Parties that handle animals

    Birthday programs and education visits. Book ahead. These fill.

    The Harris story

    From a house of tanks to Route 44.

    1940s · West Hartford

    Seth starts with fish

    A breeding hobby, then New Britain Avenue, then a teaching career and the military closed the first shop.

    1999 · Bloomfield

    Reopened next to a bar

    Four hundred square feet, then 1,800 next door. Six years of the modern shop.

    Avon

    Two village rooms

    Old Avon Village, then street level. The collection outgrew both.

    Now · Canton

    364 Albany Turnpike

    Parking, access, road frontage, and room to be a fish-and-reptile shop again.
